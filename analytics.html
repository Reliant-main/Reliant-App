How the numbers work

  • Revenue is the sale price. Net is after platform fees (TikTok 8%; Whatnot 11% + $0.30; eBay 10%). Each row in the Sales Master Roster counts as one book sold.
  • Profit, average profit and margin use only sales with a cost recorded. Margin is profit ÷ net, matching the Sales Master Roster. Used sales with no cost of their own take the cost of their source on the Sources tab ($0.49 for HPB).
  • Dates: the presets count back from today; the Dates button picks any days. Arrows compare with your compare dates when set, otherwise with the same number of days just before.
  • Sell-through is books sold ÷ books shown. It only counts shows whose R’s were recorded (shows sent from the Workspace, and used shows in Show Summary). Main counts sales before the speed round; final adds it. An R later sold in the same show isn’t counted as unsold.
  • Projection fits a straight trend line to the last 12 full weeks; the range shows where 4 out of 5 weeks have landed around it. Scenarios and “if all inventory sells” are what-if estimates, not forecasts.
  • Inventory figures (on hand, slow movers, inventory value) are new books only: the latest count plus everything received, sold, given away and adjusted since. Combined adds new and used sales together.
  • Where the data comes from: the Reliant database, copied from the Google Sheets every night around 2 a.m. and checked against them. Shows use standard names (for example 10.9.2026 Show 1).
