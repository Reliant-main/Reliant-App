# Reliant Workspace

The Reliant Distribution Workspace, rebuilt on the Reliant database (Supabase), one section at a time.

- `index.html`: sign-in and the home screen
- `analytics.html`: Sales Analytics (owners and managers)
- `inventory.html`: Inventory (costs and value shown to owners and managers only). Shows Available now and Held for shows: copies pulled for a show in Show Prep or sitting in a running show. **Count** (operators and up): count part or all of the shelf by search or scanner, good and damaged copies, undo your own entries, finish with a differences list and an optional "full count, set the rest to 0". **Adjust & damage** (receiving staff and up): adjustments with a reason, and damaged copies (sellable or written off); managers can remove recent entries.
- `orders.html`: Orders & Receiving (owners, managers and receiving staff; costs for owners and managers only). **New order** (owners and managers): drop a Book Depot .xls, Faire .csv, Texas Bookman .pdf or any supplier spreadsheet (a new layout is matched once and saved in `supplier_layouts`); checks totals and duplicates, spreads shipping into landed cost, adds new titles to the book list, and saves the order with `order_create`. **Receiving**: open a waiting order, enter what arrived (partial deliveries, First Book front-end share, damaged / can't sell, short books that may still come) and confirm with `order_receive`. **Shortages**: mark late books arrived or not coming (`short_resolve`).
- `purchasing.html`: Purchasing, the buy-list builder (owners and managers). Saved lists live in the database, since they don't come from the sheets.
- `shows.html`: Shows, view only: every show's results, customer events and open requests. The live show screen is `live.html` (rehearsal for now).
- `fulfillment.html`: Fulfillment for new, used and vintage shows (owners, managers and operators). Each show keeps its own inventory: new-book shows use new stock, used shows use used copies (a canceled used sale puts its copy back in used stock; a used giveaway takes one copy out). TikTok shows within 10 days can be ticked to work together, and **By buyer** groups every selected show's books by buyer. **Giveaways waiting for a book** lists the Ticket System's giveaway tickets for the show(s) (matched by app and day until Book Spotlight links the show) so staff assign the book. Used shows from the Used Books workbook are changed the Used Books way (kept through the nightly copy). Pick a show, cancel sales (with a 15-second Undo; the book goes back on the shelf), log giveaways (Raffle, Over-MSRP bonus, Life event, Other gift) whose cost comes off the show's profit, giveaways outside a show, and cancellations by customer. Totals update as you go. **Until switch day it only changes test shows** (setting `app_fulfillment_live` = false), because the nightly copy still rebuilds real shows from the sheets.
- `close-show.js`: the shared Close show / Reopen show window used by Shows, Fulfillment and Used Books. Closing (owners and managers) shows a summary and anything that looks off, then makes the show final: the database refuses any change to a closed show until an owner reopens it. Every close and reopen is kept in `show_close_log` with its summary.
- `used.html`: Used Books (owners and managers): load results, check-ins due, used and vintage shows. A Consignors panel shows what each consignor (8th Day 50%, Dr. Baker 60% of the after-fees amount) is owed per month, with a Record payment button. The scan station comes later.
- `live.html`: the live show screen (Book Spotlight), same layout and buttons as the current Workspace: operator view, TV window (`live.html#display`), single-book and table rounds, sets (Load next set, a Rejects set and a Celeb shots set), two-book lots, damaged copies (replaced or not), bundles, R's, used-book shows with the speed round, requests, life events, flags and operator layouts. **Real and practice shows**: Start show asks which. A practice show stays in its own store (`live_docs`) only. A real show (`real: true`) is copied by the database (trigger `spot_apply` on `live_docs`) into the real shows, sales (sale numbers, lot price and Whatnot fee split, costs, Ticket System buyer), R's, customer requests and damaged stock as each change is saved; edits and deletes update the same records, Fulfillment cancellations are kept, and anything that can't be saved is listed in `spotlight_issues` and the closing summary. The playlist's books count as Held for shows until they sell or the show ends. Until switch day (`app_fulfillment_live` = false) real shows are saved as TEST shows numbered 90+. `live-bridge.js` keeps an outbox per browser tab, so changes made while the internet is down are sent in order when it's back (a yellow note shows how many are waiting).
- `station.html`: the Ticket System: platform orders arrive live (Whatnot first) with per-show buyer numbers, chits print on the Star TSP700II (2 x 1 in, at the sale, after the scan with the title, or both), and each scanned or typed book attaches to the oldest sale without a book (+ Same sale for lots). Practice sales test it without Whatnot; they live in `live_shows`/`live_sales` marked practice and never reach real sales.
- `extension/`: the Reliant Page Reader (Chrome extension, download from the Ticket System's Sale sources). **Learning** records what an app's live page sends; **Reading** turns sales into tickets (and records for the first 10 minutes too). TikTok is ready: it reads the seller LIVE dashboard's auction results (buyer, price, payment status), keyed on the auction itself so the chit prints at the win (status 5, before TikTok makes the order) and each auction is one ticket, failed payments show as cancelled, and a stream restart within 45 minutes stays in the same show. It checks in every 20 seconds; the Ticket System shows a red bar with a sound if it stops hearing the app, and any sale on the app's list that didn't arrive is sent again. Whatnot and eBay readers are still empty.
- `extension/sidepanel.html`: the **Reliant Tickets sidebar** (opened from the extension menu). It sits beside the live page: the current show's sales newest first with buyer numbers and payment status (waiting with a countdown, paid, payment failed), **Print another chit** (the computer ticked to print chits in the Ticket System prints it), each buyer's purchases this show and on earlier shows of the **same app only** (usernames differ between apps), open requests and life events, and the reader's green/red status. Sound for failed payments and a quiet reader is off unless turned on. Works for every app: page-reader apps and apps on their official connection (Whatnot). eBay Learning (0.3.1) also records server-sent events, eBay frames, and a 5-second text snapshot of the eBay Live listing rows, because the first two eBay recordings missed the win moment.
- `prep.html`: Show Prep, building a show's playlist ahead of time (works on a tablet; camera scanning where the browser supports it). Add set starts the next table set; + 2nd book makes a two-book lot; ✂ Split here, Clear current set and Bring back last set reshape sets. Each list has a platform, and Release books returns a list's books to available stock without deleting it. The live show's "Load from Show Prep" reads these lists.
- `books.html`: Book Info: every title's author, Goodreads, spice, release date, genres, tropes, series, synopsis, notes and pronunciation; "Info needed" lists books in stock or on order that are missing what the TV shows. Owners and managers edit and add books (`book_save`, which also updates the show screen's book list).
- `executive.html`: Executive (owners only, enforced in the database): a shared calendar with a notes panel for the selected day, event types with colors (bills, payouts, platform promos, etc.), days colored for big money in or out, alerts (reminders, bills not marked paid, promos starting, significant financial days), and a quick review of recent shows (sales, profit, average sale and profit per book, by platform; 7 days by default, longer or picked dates). Its tile sits in its own Executive group at the top of the home screen, shown to owners only.
- Home screen: **Switch person** lets someone on a shared device take over by picking their name and typing their PIN (edge function `pin-switch`, 5 wrong tries locks that person for 15 minutes); **My PIN** sets your own 4–6 digit PIN.
- **Username accounts** (no email): owners and managers add them in Settings with a name, username (First.Last), starting password and optional PIN (edge function `staff-account`). They sign in with the username; behind the scenes it is `username@staff.reliantbookstore.com`, which receives no mail. Operator or Scanner only. They're asked to choose their own password on first sign-in (optional), and Settings has Reset password for them.
- `tools.html` and `tools.json`: **Computer tools** (everyone): the programs that go on the store's computers, each with a download, setup and update steps, what changed, and whether the computer you're on has the newest version. `tools.json` is the list; **raise a tool's version there whenever its download changes**, because the tools read it. The **Chit Printer** (1.1.0 and later) checks it when it starts and every 6 hours and updates itself once no chit has printed for 1 hour (the old copy is kept as `previous.ps1`; log in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ReliantChitPrinter\chit-printer.log`). The **Page Reader** (0.4.1 and later) shows its version and "Update available" in its menu, and tells this page its version. The **Scan Helper** (`scan-helper/`, 0.6.6 and later) does the same.
- `scan-helper/`: the Reliant Scan Helper (Chrome extension for the CashMonkey scanning stations). `reliant-scan-helper.zip` is the download; rebuild it when the files change.
- `chit-printer/mac/`: the Mac version of the Chit Printer (Perl, uses macOS's own printing; starts at login through a LaunchAgent; same 127.0.0.1:17861 connection, so the Ticket System needs no changes). `chit-printer/reliant-chit-printer-mac.zip` is the download; rebuild it when the files change and raise `chit-printer-mac` in `tools.json`.
- `chit-printer/`: the Reliant Chit Printer (Windows) that prints Ticket System chits with no print window. `reliant-chit-printer.zip` is the download; rebuild it when the files change.
- `settings.html`: Settings (owners and managers). Add or remove people by email, set their name and access level, and approve their sign-up. Each person sets their own password.
- `live-bridge.js`: connects the show screen to the database (shared show store with live updates, and the book list).
- `config.js`: the database address and its public key. What each person can see or change is enforced inside the database by their sign-in and role.

The site is published with GitHub Pages straight from this repository, so there is no build step. Until a section is switched over, the current Workspace (on Google Sheets) stays in use for it. A nightly copy keeps the database current with the sheets.


Database changes for these screens are in `db/2026-11-23_workspace_write_screens.sql`; the PIN switch function is in `supabase/functions/pin-switch/`. Until switch day (`app_fulfillment_live` = false) the nightly copy from the sheets replaces counts, adjustments, damaged entries and receiving on Purchase Ledger orders, so entries made in those screens before then are for trying out.
