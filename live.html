                How it works

                Shift+Enter
                Open search. Typing or scanning does nothing until search is open, so a stray scan can't change the book.
                Enter
                In search: show the highlighted book. During a playlist this is a one-off detour that leaves the playlist where it was.
                Ctrl+Enter
                In search: put the book into the playlist right after the current spot (it plays next).
                → Space PgDn
                Next book in the playlist. A presentation clicker works here. Holding the key moves only one book.
                ← PgUp
                Previous book in the playlist. After a detour, ← returns to where you were.
                Click a title
                Jump to any book in the playlist. The spot you left turns gold, with a "Back to" button. Arrows then move from the book you jumped to.
                F
                Flag the book on screen for fixing later. Flags are kept for 30 days.
                Tab
                Show or hide the playlist list for a moment, whatever the List setting is.
                Esc
                Close whatever is open.

                Two windows: this window is the Operator view. Click Open TV window and drag that window to the TV monitor, then double-click it for full screen. The TV window shows only the book and follows everything you do here. The clicker works in either window.

                Table rounds (several books under the camera): click Table rounds, set the books per round, and click Load next round to put the next books from the playlist on the table. After each auction, type the winning bid, press Enter, then press the number of the book the winner picked. A lot can hold several books: select two or more lots and click Combine into one lot, or select a lot and scan a book into it. + Next book adds one more playlist book to the table. Picked lots leave the table. When nobody buys, type R and press Enter: the books left are marked no sale and the round ends.

                Selling from the playlist: with a show running, the book on the TV is the book you're selling. Click the Price box, type the price and press Enter, or type R and press Enter if nobody bought it. The TV moves to the next playlist book on its own (untick the box under the price to stop that). For a bundle, click Bundle, then "+ Add" for the next playlist books, then type the one bundle price. Scanning a book still works and overrides the TV book for that sale.

                Sales: click Start show and pick TT, WN or EB. Click the Scan sold book box, scan, type the price, press Enter. For a bundle, click Bundle, scan 2 to 4 books, then type the one bundle price. Each book gets an even share. When the show is over, click End show and copy the rows out.

                Used-book shows: at Start show pick Used books. Set Condition and Source once (they stay set) and Genre per book if you like, then scan each book as it comes up and type the price or R. A book with no barcode can be typed by title. Click Start speed round when you re-run the unsold books, and rescan each one: speed-round sales are marked Speed, and a second R doesn't count as another reject. In this rehearsal version nothing is sent to the used workbook.

                Playlist: scan books into the box at the bottom of the list to build it. The same ISBN can be added more than once. With an empty playlist, the arrows step through your whole book list.

                List setting: Open keeps the list on screen, Auto shows it when the mouse moves, Hidden keeps it off the TV.

                Rehearsal version. This is the live show screen on the new Workspace. Shows started here are practice shows: their sales stay on this screen and never reach Analytics, Inventory, the sheets or the nightly copy. Delete a practice show from Export shows when you're done. Keep running real shows on the current Workspace until switch day.

                Your books come from the Reliant database (the nightly copy of your sheets), checked about once a minute. Use Refresh book list to pull changes right away. Load from Show Prep reads the lists built on the Show Prep page, which works on a tablet.
